# Backlink Strategy

## Principles

Earn legitimate backlinks through useful content and genuine relationships. Never buy links or use spam tactics.

## Linkable assets (current and planned)

- Question-led tailoring guides (`/blog/`)
- Garment-specific service pages with practical preparation guidance
- Future: printable measurement checklists, wedding preparation timelines, fabric selection guides

## Outreach opportunities

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Startup directories | Legitimate app discovery platforms where eligible |
| Fashion & wedding | Publications, planners, boutiques |
| Textile & fabric | Stores, educators, institutes |
| Local media | City publications when service launches are approved |
| Creators | Fashion YouTubers, tailoring educators |
| Founders | Podcasts, interviews, founder stories |

## Tactics to avoid

- Link farms, PBNs, paid undisclosed links
- Fake Reddit/Quora posts
- Mass AI guest posts
- Spam directory submissions
- Automated comment links

## Measurement

Track referring domains through Search Console after launch. Prioritize relevance and editorial quality over quantity.

## Media page

Use `/media/` for press inquiries. Do not add "As Seen In" sections without real coverage.
