Account Deletion Request

Last Updated: June 2026

Apne Tailor allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Apne Tailor app.
  2. Go to your Profile or Account settings.
  3. Select "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we may delete:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Saved addresses
  • Account preferences
  • Profile information

What Data May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained for legal, fraud prevention, security, account recovery, dispute resolution, tax, regulatory, or operational purposes.

This may include order records, transaction history, audit logs, and information required to comply with applicable laws.

Retention Period

Account deletion requests are processed according to our internal retention policies. Some information may be retained for up to 30 days after deletion request submission or longer where legally required.
